Good To Know
Frequently asked
questions.
Do you only deliver within Chattogram?
Our showroom and installation team are based in Agrabad, Chattogram, and that's where we deliver fastest. For anywhere outside Chattogram, message us on WhatsApp with your location and we'll confirm delivery options for you.
How long does a bespoke piece take to make?
It depends on the piece, the materials, and how customized the design is. After your free design consultation, our team will give you a clear timeline before you confirm anything.
What payment options do you offer?
We offer easy payment options to make bespoke furniture accessible. Ask our team on WhatsApp or in-showroom for the payment plan that works best for you.
Can I visit the showroom without an appointment?
Yes — our Agrabad showroom is open for walk-ins. That said, booking a free design consultation ahead of time means a designer will be ready to walk you through options as soon as you arrive.
Do you only make furniture, or help with interior styling too?
Both. Heaven Furniture Mart designs and crafts the furniture itself, and our team can also guide you on how each piece fits into your overall room styling — from layout to finish.
